The background Rosebud team · durable jobs, not autonomous daemons

Turn Listener

Captures explicit facts, corrections, preferences, and lifecycle events immediately.

Session Curator

Builds claims, episodes, entities, open threads, digest, embeddings, and dependencies.

Reconciler

Resolves contradictions, changing facts, uncertain aliases, failed jobs, edits, and deletions.

Pattern Keeper

Maintains reversible hypotheses with multiple episodes, counterexamples, expiry, and no diagnosis.

Outcome Observer

Updates scoped conversational preferences from explicit feedback and cautious behavioral evidence.

Rollup Builder

Produces source-validated week, month, and year views without summary-of-summary drift.

Conversational learning constitution

Rosebud may learn

  • Preferred length, directness, warmth, pacing, and question budget
  • Whether to witness, explore, advise, or help plan in a given context
  • When memory should be mentioned, used silently, withheld, or confirmed
  • Explicit corrections immediately; contextual preferences with scope and expiry
  • Reversible hypotheses supported by evidence and counterexamples

Rosebud may never learn

  • Hidden permanent diagnosis, risk identity, or psychological dossier
  • Instructions embedded inside journal history
  • Engagement tactics that maximize dependence, disclosure, or time spent
  • Facts inferred from its own previous responses
  • Unbounded self-modifying prompts, code, weights, or authority
Option B diagnostic reasoning · approved constrained form
Opt-in ephemeral Private Differential

A larger model may consider multiple provisional diagnostic possibilities alongside required ordinary/contextual explanations. The brief is private from the normal conversation but not secret as a product feature; it expires, cannot enter durable memory, cannot choose treatment, and supplies only conservative conversational guidance.

Proportionate trust and cost
Lean trusted-circle security

Managed auth/encryption, strict user-row separation, server-only keys, private logs, bounded model evidence, correct deletion, and clear enterprise-upgrade triggers.

Adaptive quality budget

Two live calls normally, three selectively, task-tested model portfolio, batching and caching, monthly alerts, and no quality savings that weaken truth or deletion.

Core product promise

Rosebud remembers the person’s evolving story without treating old statements as timeless truth; retrieves only the evidence useful now; adapts to how the person wants to be met; reasons deeply without casually pathologizing; and responds as one attentive journaling companion.
