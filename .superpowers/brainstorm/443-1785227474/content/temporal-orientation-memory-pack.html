Clock · ~60 tokens
Exact local date/time, weekday, timezone, week boundaries, and current session duration.
Identity core · 250–350
Preferred name, pronouns, important people, current roles, durable interests, and explicit boundaries.
Interaction policy · 200–300
Length, question budget, advice mode, tone, pacing, memory-mention posture, scope, and confidence.

Present work, relationships, active concerns, priorities, and recent changes—with validity and sources.
Today / recent · 300–450
High-resolution recent events and conversation continuity.
This week · 450–650
Week-to-date episodes, mood/context shifts, successes, tensions, and active threads.
Last week · 350–550
Previous completed calendar week, emphasizing unfinished connections to now.
Two-week delta · 200–350
What changed between the periods—not a third repetition of both summaries.
This month · 350–550
Broader month-to-date direction, older episodes, recurring themes, and counterexamples.
Open threads · 200–350
Upcoming, unresolved, postponed, completed, dismissed, and do-not-nag state.
Changes & uncertainty · 150–250
Superseded facts, ambiguous people/dates, corrections, and claims Rosebud must not assert.

The assembler applies source-level deduplication and allocates tokens by marginal information gain. An episode already represented in “today” is referenced—not retold—in “this week.”

User summary · current snapshot, not a frozen biography

Always-on user context

  • Explicit identity and preferred form of address
  • Important people with current relationships and aliases
  • Current roles, projects, living context, and active priorities
  • Durable likes, dislikes, routines, boundaries, and goals
  • Recent meaningful changes and their effective dates

Excluded from the core summary

  • Private differential or diagnostic possibilities
  • Low-confidence personality or trauma interpretations
  • Every painful episode merely because it was salient
  • Superseded facts presented without historical labels
  • Assistant-written descriptions of the user
Cache and freshness behavior
Precomputed
Identity, preference, week, fortnight, month, and open-thread projections update in background.
Per-turn assembly
Software combines cached blocks, exact clock, current session, and retrieved evidence. No extra LLM call.
Invalidation
New message, correction, deletion, timezone change, preference edit, and finished job bump block versions.
Stale fallback
Mark stale, trust exact sources, omit uncertain synthesis, and let tools fetch current evidence.

A context-budget resolver trims lower-value temporal detail before it removes identity, explicit preferences, corrections, critical evidence, or generation reserve. Near the model limit, older conversation turns compact into a source-linked session summary.

Temporal-context amendment

Should this become an explicit part of the approved architecture?

A

Always-on 3–5k temporal orientation pack + tools — recommended

Exact clock, identity, preferences, current-life snapshot, non-overlapping week/fortnight/month views, open threads, uncertainty, and turn-specific evidence on every conversational turn.

B

Minimal core + tools on demand

Inject only clock, identity, and preferences; let Rosebud fetch all temporal summaries when it notices the need. Cheaper, but less consistently oriented.

C

Inject every full rollup independently

Today, week, fortnight, and month summaries are concatenated as-is; simplest, but repetitive, biased, and vulnerable to summary drift.
