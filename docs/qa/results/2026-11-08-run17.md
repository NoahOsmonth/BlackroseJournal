# QA run 17 — letters to your future self (#11)

A letter you write now that stays sealed until a day you choose. Pure local: no network, no AI, no new dependency.

## Reached from Settings

Settings → **Your data** → **Letters to your future self** → `/letters`.

## What the writer sees

| Situation | Screen |
|---|---|
| No letters | "No sealed letters yet. Write one and pick the day it should open." |
| Just sealed | "Sealed. It opens on the day you picked, and not before." |
| A sealed letter | **Sealed letter** / "Opens in 3 months · written 8 Nov 2026" / Delete |
| A letter whose day came | "Written 8 Nov 2026" and the words themselves |
| Can't be sealed | "Write something first, even a line." · "Pick the day this should open." · "A letter needs a day in the future to open." |

Dates are always spelled out ("14 Feb 2027"), never a raw stored key.

## The rule that matters

A sealed letter gives away **only** how long the wait is. `toListEntries` omits `body` entirely for a sealed letter, so the words are not in the rendered data at all — not hidden with opacity or clipped.

Verified live:

1. Wrote a letter with the words *"Dear future me — the kitchen leak is fixed…"*, unlock day 2027-02-14, and pressed **Seal it**.
2. Screen then read "Sealed letter · Opens in 3 months · written 8 Nov 2026". Searching the whole page for *kitchen leak* returned **false**.
3. Storage held `{"schemaVersion":1,"letters":[{…"unlockDate":"2027-02-14","body":<81 chars>}]}`.
4. Moved the stored unlock day to 2020-01-01 and reloaded: the letter appeared under **Opened** with its words showing. So the seal opens on the day and not before.
5. Test letter removed afterwards; the screen is back to the empty state.

## Engineering notes

- One key, `@blackrose_letters`, behind the account-scoped adapter; every mutation runs through a single queue, so two seals at once cannot drop each other (this is tested, not assumed).
- Corrupt payloads return an empty list; a malformed row is dropped without taking the good ones with it.
- Unlock comparison is plain date-key ordering, so a letter opens at the start of its day wherever the writer is — the same day rule the rest of the app uses.

## Not done

- **Typing the day is manual.** The field takes `YYYY-MM-DD`; there is no date picker. A picker is the next obvious improvement, and would make "two months out" a suggestion rather than a default to overwrite.
- **Nothing notifies you when a letter opens.** That belongs with reminders (#9).
- **Clear history does not clear letters yet.** `clearLetters()` exists for that and is deliberately unwired — a sealed letter is arguably something the writer does not want erased by the same button that clears entries, so that is a product decision, not an oversight. Flagged on the card.
