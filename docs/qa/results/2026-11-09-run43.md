## Gap survey → choice

The 2026 checklist is largely **already covered**: smart prompts (`utils/guidedPrompts.ts`), AI follow-ups (chat), mood tracking (`utils/moodStrip.ts`), privacy (local-only), memory capture (`utils/onThisDay.ts` — Day One's 2026 "On This Day" already exists here), review tools (starred entries, Archive search), weekly summaries (`utils/thisWeek.ts` + week letter), reminders (card #9), and the calm interface. Streak calendar, rewards, and the 53-week year grid also exist.

**Missing: any monthly review** — `grep monthly / monthlyReview` = 0 hits. That is checklist item 7 and a low-risk, fully local slice: no native build, no AI key, no network, no new storage key.

## What shipped

| Layer | File |
|---|---|
| Pure builder | `utils/monthlyReview.ts` — `buildMonthlyReview(records, year, month, today)`; Sunday-first calendar, stats, longest run, mood words by distinct days (emoji stripped), recurring themes via the existing `collectWeeklyThemes` |
| Hook | `hooks/insights/useMonthlyReview.ts` — one `useMemo` over entries + check-ins already loaded (R2, no new read) |
| Screen | `app/monthly-review.tsx` — 206 lines, prev/next month, three plain stats, 36px calendar, moods, themes, empty states |
| Route / link | `app/_layout.tsx`, `app/(tabs)/insights.tsx` — "See a month in review" |

All dates are local `YYYY-MM-DD`. `today` is passed in, never read inside the builder. Out-of-month records are ignored so a bad timestamp cannot invent a square.

**Bug caught by the tests before shipping:** `formatMonthLabel(2026, 11)` returned *December 2026* because JS `Date` wants month 0–11 while callers store 1–12. Fixed with `month - 1` and pinned by the test.
