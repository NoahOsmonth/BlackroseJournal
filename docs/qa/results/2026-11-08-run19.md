# QA run 19 — guided prompts (#13)

A small shelf of starting points for the writer who opened the app with nothing ready.

## Where it is

Today, directly under the writing card: **This morning · In my body · Something good**

Tapping one opens the chat with that opening line **already in the writing slip**.

## The design choice that matters

A prompt **seeds** the input, it does not send. The writer still has to write the rest of the sentence and press send. Tapping "In my body" gives you:

> Right now my body feels | ▍

An empty app is intimidating; an app that writes your entry for you is worse. This sits between: it removes the blank page without removing the writer.

## How the shelf is chosen

- Prompts that fit the hour come first (morning prompts in the morning, evening ones after 5pm).
- Anytime prompts fill the rest.
- The shelf rotates by day, so it is not the same three every time — but it is **stable within a day**, so it does not reshuffle while the writer looks at it.

## Live verified

1. Opened `/today` in the running app. Shelf rendered with three chips: `This morning`, `In my body`, `Something good`.
2. Tapped **In my body** (a full pointer sequence).
3. Route became `/chat` and the input read exactly `"Right now my body feels"`.
4. The line was **not** in the transcript — nothing was sent.

Measured layout in both themes: three chips 109/98/129 px wide inside a 400 px shelf, no horizontal overflow, no page scroll, identical in light and dark.

## A defect this round caught

The chips shipped at **33 px tall** — below the 44 pt touch floor this repo already documents (and uses in `EntryInsightsCard`). Raised to `min-h-[44px]` and re-measured: **44 px**.

## Copy fix

The route param originally carried an insight's wording — *"The user tapped an insight about this topic"* — which would have told the AI the wrong thing about how the writer arrived. Rewritten for a starting point, with an explicit instruction not to parrot the opening line back.

## Not done

- The library has 12 prompts and no browse-all screen. A full list with search belongs with the wider prompt work; the shelf deliberately shows only three.
- Prompts are not personalised (no memory or history drives the choice yet) — rotation is by date and hour only. That keeps it deterministic and testable, but a prompt drawn from what the writer has been circling would be stronger. Worth its own card.
- Once the writer sends, the chat behaves normally; there is no "you started from a prompt" marker.
