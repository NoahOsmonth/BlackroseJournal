## The gap found by zoom-in QA

`app/(tabs)/entries.tsx` already had free-text search, a starred filter, topic chips (round 39) and mood chips (round 41) - but NO way to reach a date. A writer who wants "what did I write on March 3rd" had to scroll the whole history or guess words. Round 43 added day-tap to A month in review (an Insights screen), not to the Archive itself. Day One 2026 says "tap on any day to reveal the journal entry you crafted on that very occasion".
