## The gap

The Insights card read:

> On 3 of your 4 lighter days, People was in the mix.

Two problems, both about the reader, not the code:

- **Of what period?** The card looks at 28 days and never says so. "4 days" could
  be the last month or the whole journal.
- **A month is the only lens.** A pattern that only appears across a season is
  invisible, and the fixed window drops it silently.

## The change

- "Looking at the last four weeks." on the card.
- Two lenses, four weeks (default) and three months.
- The lens is a **view choice**, not a saved setting: no new storage key, no
  preference to explain later. Both lenses call the same pure function, so the
  evidence rules (4-day minimum, clear lean, counts not percentages) cannot
  differ between them.
