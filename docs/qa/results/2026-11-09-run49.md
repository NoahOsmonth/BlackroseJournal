## The gap found by zoom-in QA

`app/monthly-review.tsx` rendered a 7-column calendar with 36px filled squares for days with writing, but every square was a plain `View` with NO `onPress`. They looked tappable and were not. Day One's equivalent view opens that day's writing. `app/writing-year.tsx` already had a working day-tap pattern, but the month view never got it.
