# QA run 70 - 2026-11-09 - the photo on the shared card (#107)

Round 66.

## Why this round existed

#106 shipped a photo but the share export still drew only words. An illustrated day
exported as a text card. Closing the loop you just opened is cheaper than starting a
new one, and the gap was already written down rather than forgotten.

## What shipped

- The card builder takes an optional photo and draws it above the words, clipped to
  rounded corners, filled with preserveAspectRatio slice so it never distorts.
- The card grows by exactly the photo plus a 40px gap.
- Height capped at 640 px: a 1200x800 landscape draws 563 px tall, a 600x2400
  portrait is capped instead of pushing the text off the card.
- A photo with no readable size draws nothing.
- No photo means byte-identical output to before.

## QA: prove the pixels, not the markup

A markup assertion can pass while the renderer draws nothing. So the check sampled
the exported PNG itself.

| measure | value |
|---|---|
| exported PNG | 1080 x 1367 |
| photo colour in | rgb(200, 40, 90) |
| sampled inside photo block | rgb(201, 40, 90) |
| sampled below (card) | rgb(27, 26, 24) |
| distinct tones | 121 |

The one-unit difference is JPEG re-encoding at 0.72, which is expected. A card that
merely referenced the photo would show the surface tone there instead.

## One thing the tests caught

The first cap (520 px) was low enough that a normal 3:2 landscape photo hit it too,
which made the portrait test indistinguishable from the landscape test. Raised to
640 so only genuinely tall photos are capped.

## Tests

5 new:
- no image element when the entry has no photo
- the photo is drawn above the words and clipped
- the card grows by exactly the photo plus the gap (563 + 40)
- a tall portrait is capped (640 + 40)
- an unreadable size draws nothing

Sabotage: drop the photo block from the markup -> 1 failed.
