## Trap of this round

Two probe attempts reported the SAME colour in both schemes, which reads as a
dark-mode bug. It was not:

1. `user-theme-preference` is the real key, and the **default is dark**, so a
   probe that never sets it measures dark twice and calls it "light and dark".
2. `theme` and `color-scheme` look like plausible keys and are inert.

**Rule:** confirm a scheme flip by watching a token that must differ (the line
colour), and treat "both schemes identical" as a probe failure until the key is
proven to work.
