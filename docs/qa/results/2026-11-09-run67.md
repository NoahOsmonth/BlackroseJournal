# QA run 67 - 2026-11-09 - the last thirty days as a strip (#104)

Round 63.

## What shipped

A quiet strip on the day-feeling card: one thin bar per day, oldest on the left,
tone on a single lighter -> heavier ramp, a gap kept as a faint outline.

A ramp rather than five unrelated hues, because the moods already mean "how heavy
was today" and a ramp says that without a legend. Never colour-only: every bar
carries its own words.

## Two defects I found in my own work

Both were found by measuring the rendered page, not by reading the code.

**1. The palest feeling looked like a gap.**

| | measured |
|---|---|
| first ramp step (light) | rgb(231, 223, 207) |
| empty-bar border (light) | rgb(226, 220, 210) |

Nine units apart. A "Light" day read as a missing day. Fixed two ways: the first
step deepened to rgb(217, 201, 172), and gaps became fainter (opacity 0.4) -
solid fill vs faint outline carries the distinction that tone could not.

**2. The strip drew itself empty for a frame.**

It had its own useDayMoodDays() subscription, which resolved a tick after the
card's, so the card appeared with no strip and the strip arrived late. The card now
holds one subscription and passes the days down; useDayMoodInsight takes days
instead of reading them. One read, one frame.

## Tests

- 5 pure-builder cases: order ends on today, gaps kept, ramp order 1..5, days
  outside the strip and unknown ids ignored, month boundary crossed without a skip.
- 2 in the card test: 30 bars with correct labels; no strip when nothing is
  recorded.
- Sabotage: drop the newest day -> 4 failed.

Unknown mood ids normalize to "no entry" so a stored leftover can never print a raw
id on screen (rule 13).
