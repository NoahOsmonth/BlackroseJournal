## Gap survey → choice

`tagFilter 0`, `filterByTag 0`, `autoTag 0`, `hashtag 0`. The Archive already had **full-text search** and a **starred-only filter**, but no way to *browse* by topic. `JournalEntry.analysis.topics` exists but is optional and AI-dependent, so the chips are derived **purely from the words already in the feed** — zero effort, zero new storage key, zero AI call, zero network, zero native build.

### Design decisions the tests enforced

1. **A word repeated 9 times in one entry must not outrank a word in 2 entries.** `topicWordsForItem` returns a `Set`; counts are distinct items, not mentions. Sabotage (per-character counting) turned **3 tests red**; exact inverse restored **23 green**.
2. **A chip's number must survive search + starred filters.** `countTopicItems(beforeTopicSections, chip.label)` recounts against the narrowed feed; a pure test asserts `garden` goes from 2 to 0 after a `coffee` filter.
3. **Empty day headings disappear** rather than leaving a bare date, matching `filterStarredSections`.
4. **Ties break alphabetically** so the row never jitters between renders. A test's own first expectation ("garden first") was wrong and was corrected to the honest tie-break.

### Two real UX bugs live QA caught

- **`than · 4` was a browsable topic.** The stop list had `then`/`their` but not `than`. Added it, pinned with a unit test (16 total); live re-check shows a grammar-clean row.
- **The first scroll probe measured the wrapper and reported `rowScrollable:false`.** Measuring the actual scroller showed `948px` of content in `448px` with `overflowX:auto`. Pinned by a wiring assertion on `contentContainerStyle`. Rounds 36/37/38 lesson: measure the rendered rectangle, not the name.
