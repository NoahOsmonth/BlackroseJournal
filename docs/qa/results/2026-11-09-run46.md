## Gap survey → choice

The app already had a **partial** version: `utils/onThisDay.ts` `pickOnThisDay()` returns ONE oldest entry, `hooks/today/useOnThisDay.ts` binds it, and `components/today/OnThisDayRow.tsx` shows one quiet row on Today. `throwback / anniversary / pastYears / sameDay = 0` hits, and there was **no On This Day route at all**. 2026 products give a FULL VIEW: every past year, grouped by year, tappable, with a way to step to other dates. The single-row version cannot show two memories from different years on the same date.

### Design decisions the tests enforced

1. **Every past year, not one.** Grouping into year buckets. Sabotage restricting the builder to a single year turned **1 test red**; exact inverse restored **25 green**.
2. **Multiple entries in the same year** are all kept, newest first — the old one-pick row could not do this.
3. **Never invent a leap-day match.** Feb 29 in a non-leap year simply has no matches; stepping across a leap day is pure date math.
4. **Stepping has no clock read inside the util**; the screen takes one clock read on mount and steps from its own date key.
