# QA run 21 — reminders, part one (#9)

Reminders are a two-part feature: **decide** when to nudge and what to say, then **ask the OS** to deliver it. This round finishes and verifies the first part and leaves the second as one clearly-marked seam.

## What works today

Settings → **Reminders**:

| Row | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Morning nudge | On/off, defaults 8:00 AM, hour choices from 5 AM to 10 AM |
| Evening nudge | On/off, defaults 9:00 PM, hour choices from 5 PM to 10 PM |
| Skip if I already wrote | On by default — no nudge on days you already wrote |

The section header summarises the schedule: **"Morning at 8:00 AM · evening at 9:00 PM"**, or **"Off"**.

## What the rules decide

`utils/reminders.ts` answers three questions and is fully tested:

- **Should it fire today?** No if the nudge is off, no if the writer already wrote that slot (when the skip rule is on), and **no if the time has already passed** — scheduling a past time would either fire instantly or be silently dropped, and neither is what the writer asked for.
- **At what time?** Local hours and minutes, compared as one number so the clock is not special-cased.
- **What does it say?** "What wants your attention today?" in the morning, "How did today sit with you?" in the evening.

## Live verified

1. Settings showed the row as **"Reminders, Off"**.
2. Opened the section: both nudges Off, skip rule On.
3. Tapped **Morning nudge** → hour choices appeared (5:00 AM … 10:00 AM), the row flipped to **On**, and the summary became **"Morning at 8:00 AM"**.
4. Storage held `{"schemaVersion":1,"settings":{"morningEnabled":true,"morning":{"hour":8,"minute":0},…}}`.
5. Cleared it and reloaded: back to **"Reminders, Off"**.

## Why nothing is delivered yet

`services/reminders/reminderScheduler.ts` is the seam. It does **not** call a scheduler, and the file says why in full:

- Local scheduled notifications need `expo-notifications` plus a native build.
- This repo is managed/Expo Go — no `android/` or `ios/` directory — and the package is not in the lockfile.
- Installing it means editing a lockfile, which AGENTS.md puts out of bounds, and the result could not be verified here: scheduled local notifications do not fire in Expo Go on Android.

So `applyPlan` returns what it *would* have scheduled (`didSchedule: false`) rather than pretending. The Settings screen, the rules and the storage are real; only delivery is missing.

**To finish:** add the dependency on a branch allowed to touch the lockfile, register the plugin in `app.json`, replace the body of `applyPlan` with cancel-all-then-schedule. Nothing else changes.
