# QA run 74 - 2026-11-09 - the year map was 371 buttons a thumb could not hit

Round 70.

## The blind spot, again

Round 69 showed the audit reporting clean because it measured nothing. Acting on that
lesson: **eleven screens existed that the sweep had never opened** - ask-rosebud,
happiness-recipe, letters, monthly-review, rewards, saved-insights, streak-haiku,
suggestions, writing-goal, writing-year.

A source guard now fails any route in app/ that is neither swept nor explicitly
exempted with a stated reason, so a new screen cannot escape the audit again.

## What the full sweep found

**742 defects on one screen.** The writing-year heatmap drew **371 pressable 14px
squares** - genuine buttons, but a fingertip is wider than three cells.

Enlarging 371 targets guarantees mis-taps. Shrinking them further is how round 35 made
them 4x4 and invisible. The affordance was wrong, not the size.

## The fix

- The year is a **picture** (role=image; labels kept for screen readers).
- The day is chosen with **two 44px steppers**, one day at a time, clamped to the drawn
  range. utils/dayStep.ts handles month, year and leap-day ends.
- The chat composer's photo and voice controls are real **44x44 boxes**, not 22px
  glyphs leaning on hitSlop.

## Two guards disagreed and were right

writingYearWiring and iconControlTargets both failed: they asserted the old
**mechanism** rather than the old **intent**. Both now assert the intent and were
sabotage-checked red then green.
