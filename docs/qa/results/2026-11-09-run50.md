## The gap found by zoom-in QA

`utils/guidedPrompts.ts` has 12 one-line starters and even exports `promptsByMood` for a browse list, but `hooks/journal/useGuidedPrompts.ts` shows ONLY 3 per day in a horizontal shelf on Today. A writer who wants a gratitude starter on a Tuesday cannot reach it; nothing can be kept for later. Day One 2026 ships curated themed packs + favorites + a quick-access tab.
