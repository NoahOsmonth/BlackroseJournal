# QA run 15 — what kept coming back this week (#10)

The weekly recap. No AI, no network: it counts the week the writer already wrote.

## What it does

On **Goals & Habits → Today**, above the list, a quiet card:

> **This week**
> **What kept coming back**
> Work — on 3 days
> Sleep — on 2 days
> Written on 5 days

A word only counts when it appears on **two different days**. One long entry repeating a word is one long thought, not a pattern. Words are folded so *walks* and *walk* are the same theme, and a tiny alias list joins natural pairs (*slept* → sleep, *worried* → worry, *anxious* → anxiety).

## When it says nothing

| Week | Card shows |
|---|---|
| Two or more days of writing, no repeated word | "No word came up twice yet. A few more days and the pattern will show." |
| Empty week | "Nothing written this week yet. Anything you write shows up here." |
| Exactly one day written | Nothing at all — a recap of one day is just that day |
| Reading fails | The empty week, never an error |

## Live check

Then three throwaway entries sharing real themes across three days. The card read:

The throwaway entries were removed after the check.
