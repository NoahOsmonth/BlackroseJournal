## What changed

- The Archive has a **Pick** toggle. While picking, a tap on an entry picks it
  instead of opening it, and a check appears on the row.
- A bar above the dock says how many are picked, with **Add label** and **Done**.
- Add label opens a sheet that says exactly what will happen ("This adds one
  label to 3 entries."), then labels all of them in one go.
- Check-in rows still open normally: labels belong to entries, so there is
  nothing to pick there.

## The important part: one write, not one per entry

`addEntryTagToMany(entryIds, label)` runs inside the same serialized mutation
as every other tag write: one read, one `setItem`, one notification. Tagging 12
entries one at a time would have been 12 read-modify-write cycles on one
AsyncStorage key — the exact interleaving AGENTS.md rule 4 exists to prevent.

## A trap worth writing down

An RN-Web `Modal` **stays in the DOM after it closes** — the node remains, with
`pointer-events: none` all the way up. Reading "is the sheet still open?" with a
captured element reference therefore reports "open" forever. Ask the live DOM
and check `pointer-events`, not just presence: `SHEET_INERT=true` is the real
proof that Cancel worked.
